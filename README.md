# MathWro QOL

Quality-of-life tweaks for WoW Retail and WoW Forever. Configure features with `/mqol`; adjust tracker position and appearance in Blizzard Edit Mode. Client-exclusive features appear only on the relevant client.

## General

- **Game Menu:** resize and drag the Escape menu, with a position reset.
- **Cooldown Manager shortcuts:** add a Game Menu button and optional slash-command shortcuts.
- **Auction House filters:** automatically enable current-expansion and usable-item filters.
- **Hearthstone destination:** show the destination zone in its tooltip after observing a bind at an inn.
- **Combat logging:** automatically log selected instance types, with optional maximum-level gating.
- **Camera distance:** optionally restore the client's maximum camera zoom.
- **Spell Queue Window:** capture and preserve your preferred queue window.
- **Edit Mode nudge:** position frames precisely with arrow buttons and a coordinate readout.

## Trackers

- **Combat Tracker:** display racial, on-use trinket, and consumable cooldowns with configurable groups, ordering, text, exclusions, and Masque skinning.
- **Food & Water:** automatically select usable bag refreshments, with mage-food preference, buff-food filtering, optional clickable icons, a separate display, and managed food/water macros.
- **External Tracker:** show duration icons, play selected sounds, or both when configured buffs are applied to you. Available spells depend on the client.
- **Bloodlust Tracker — Retail:** independently track Bloodlust variants and drums with a duration icon.

## Other Addon Tweaks

- **ElvUI:** keep selected action bars visible during vehicle/override states and color health bars for configured player-cast buffs.
- **EllesmereUI:** keep selected action bars visible during vehicle/override states and add precise Unlock Mode nudge controls.
- **Centered Cooldown Manager:** enable Masque skinning for Essential, Utility, and Buff icon viewers.
- **Heroic Strike companion — Retail:** show an Arms proc icon beside native or EllesmereUI cooldown buff icons, with placement and glow options.
- **PallyPower auto-hide — Forever:** hide the buff bar when your assigned blessings are confirmed, reveal it when rebuffing is needed, and preserve access in combat.

## Folio Equipment Sets — Retail

Save full Omnium Folio setups to equipment sets and restore them when switching gear. Saved-stat badges and attached Save, Apply, and Clear controls help manage each setup.
